Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how TileNova collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect basic user information to deliver, maintain, and enhance our services and customer support. This may include your name, email address, phone number, location, and specific tile inquiry details submitted through our contact forms.

The information collected helps us provide an exceptional experience, deliver accurate product catalogs and quotes, and communicate important updates regarding orders or new collections.

2. Data Protection & Security

All your information is stored securely with industry-standard safeguards and is never sold, rented, or traded to any third party. We only share information with trusted delivery partners or authorized personnel when necessary for fulfilling your order, customer support, or legal compliance.

3. Refund, Returns & Cancellation Policy

Tile orders and subscription services can be reviewed or cancelled in accordance with our showroom terms. You may request cancellation or exchange within 2 days of invoice issuance for standard stocked materials in undamaged and sealed boxes.

Refund or return requests will be inspected by our warehouse team to ensure goods are intact in their original condition. Custom imported or specially ordered tiles may be subject to manufacturer terms.

4. Communications & Opt-In

When submitting an inquiry or providing your phone number, you may receive SMS, WhatsApp, or phone assistance regarding your tile requirements and project specifications. You may opt out of promotional messages at any time.
